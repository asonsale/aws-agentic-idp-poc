Risk Department Wire Transfer Policy

Scope: This policy applies to all wire transfer requests originated by accounts under the Risk Department tenant (risk_dept_01).

Daily Transfer Limits
Risk Department accounts are capped at $50,000 per day in total outbound wire transfers. Any single transfer or cumulative daily total that would exceed $50,000 requires Risk Committee sign-off before execution.

Risk Categorization Thresholds
Accounts are classified into one of three risk categories based on transaction history and balance profile:
- LOW_RISK: Account has a clean transaction history, balance under $100,000, and no flagged activity in the past 12 months.
- MEDIUM_RISK: Account has moderate transaction volume or a balance between $100,000 and $500,000.
- HIGH_RISK: Account has flagged activity, unusually large or frequent transfers, or a balance exceeding $500,000.

Approval Workflow
1. Any wire transfer request is first checked against the account's remaining daily limit.
2. If the requested amount plus the amount already transferred today exceeds the $50,000 daily cap, the transfer is automatically declined and logged for Risk Committee review.
3. If the transfer is within the daily cap, it is recorded atomically against the account's running daily total to prevent double-spending under concurrent requests.
4. HIGH_RISK accounts require a secondary manual review regardless of transfer amount.

Escalation Contacts
Transfers requiring Risk Committee sign-off should be escalated to the on-call risk officer within one business day of the decline.
