Finance Department Wire Transfer Policy

Scope: This policy applies to all wire transfer requests originated by accounts under the Finance Department tenant (finance_dept_01).

Daily Transfer Limits
Finance Department accounts are capped at $150,000 per day in total outbound wire transfers, reflecting the department's higher transaction volume for vendor payments and payroll processing. Any single transfer or cumulative daily total that would exceed $150,000 requires CFO sign-off before execution.

Risk Categorization Thresholds
Finance Department accounts use the same three-tier classification as other departments:
- LOW_RISK: balance under $200,000, no flagged activity in the past 12 months.
- MEDIUM_RISK: balance between $200,000 and $750,000, or moderate transaction volume tied to recurring vendor payments.
- HIGH_RISK: balance exceeding $750,000, or irregular payment patterns outside the standard payroll and vendor cycle.

Approval Workflow
1. Every wire transfer request is checked against the account's remaining daily limit before execution.
2. If the requested amount plus the amount already transferred today would exceed the $150,000 daily cap, the transfer is automatically declined and routed to CFO review.
3. Approved transfers are recorded atomically against the account's running daily total, preventing two concurrent requests from both passing the limit check.
4. Recurring vendor payments under $10,000 are exempt from secondary review but still count toward the daily cap.

Escalation Contacts
Transfers requiring CFO sign-off should be escalated to the Finance Controller within one business day of the decline.
